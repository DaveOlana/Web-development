About Tenderlillies

Our History

Established with a profound commitment to educational excellence, Tenderlillies began as a vision to create a nurturing environment where foundational learning is prioritized. Over the years, we have evolved into a premier institution recognized for shaping young minds and instilling lifelong core values.

Our journey is marked by continuous growth, driven by a dedicated faculty and a community of parents who share our belief that early childhood and primary education are the absolute cornerstones of future success.

Our Mission

To provide a stimulating, safe, and academically rigorous environment that encourages holistic development, critical thinking, and a profound love for learning in every student.

Our Vision

To be a leading center of early childhood and primary education in Nigeria, recognized globally for producing well-rounded, morally upright, and globally competitive future leaders.
