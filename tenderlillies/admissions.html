Admissions

How to Apply

We seek to admit students who will benefit from our rigorous curriculum and contribute positively to the Tenderlillies community. Our admission process is transparent, thorough, and designed to ensure a mutual fit between the school and the family.

1

Purchase an Application Form

Application forms can be obtained directly from the school's administrative office during standard working hours. A non-refundable application fee applies.

2

Submit Documentation

Return the completed form along with the candidate's birth certificate, recent passport photographs, and immunization records (for Nursery applicants).

3

Entrance Evaluation

Candidates for Primary 1 through 6 will undergo a formal assessment in Numeracy and Literacy to determine appropriate placement. Nursery candidates will have an informal play-based observation.

4

Interview & Offer

Successful candidates and their parents will be invited for a brief interview with the Director, after which formal letters of admission will be issued.

Core Admission Requirements

  • Completed Application Form
  • Photocopy of Birth Certificate
  • Two (2) recent colored passport photographs
  • Medical Report / Immunization Records
  • Last academic term's report card (for Primary transfer students)
